Start your child's learning journey with us.

Admission Process

  1. Make an enquiry with the school.
  2. Discuss your child's age, class and learning needs.
  3. Complete the application and provide required documents.
  4. Attend an assessment or family meeting where applicable.
  5. Receive admission guidance and next steps from the school.

What to Prepare

  • Child's basic information and previous school details
  • Relevant identification and academic records
  • Parent/guardian contact information
